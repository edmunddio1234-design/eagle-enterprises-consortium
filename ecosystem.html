Who Does What

The four entities and their lanes

Eagle Enterprises Consortium, L3C — coordination & enterprise

The umbrella you are visiting now. EEC coordinates the network, aligns mission with sustainable social enterprise, and maintains accountability across the ecosystem. It houses Net-WIRCing Solutions, LLC and M-TOC™, and delivers subscription services, bootcamp technical assistance and training, and the SEISMIC™ incubator. You are here.

Lighthouse Community Development Corporation — stabilization & housing

The 501(c)(3)-aligned community development corporation, with roots circa 2003. Transitional housing pathways, benefits navigation, and coordinated direct services; leads real estate and infrastructure including the Greensburg site and the Innovation Complex. Visit the Lighthouse main site.

RV3 — Rural Veterans Vanguard of Valor Foundation — philanthropy

The philanthropic arm. Honors service and channels donor and grant support to rural veteran programs — the funding heart that keeps high-need pathways underwritten. Visit the RV3 site.

Guardian Angels Enterprises, L3C — advocacy, volunteers & communications

The ecosystem’s communications hub — website, app, podcast, bot, newsletter, blog, and social media — and its volunteer mobilization engine. Also stewards the Guardian Light Veterans Legacy Memorial: “Preserving the Legacy. Restoring the Light.” Visit the Guardian Angels site.

One Referral System

How a veteran moves through the network

The consortium’s founding plan describes the flow plainly: a veteran may start at Mobile Triage, move into TOWED job training and Incubator workshops, and then launch a venture the Strategic Investment team helps fund. Behind that journey sits a single referral system connecting every entity — with CAPE™, Comprehensive Assessment, Planning & Empowerment, coordinating warm hand-offs to VA clinics, mental health providers, legal clinics, universities, SBA Veteran Business Outreach Centers, and employers.

Cross-entity discipline is contractual, not aspirational: MOUs, referral-network performance expectations, consent-to-share workflows, and closed-loop referral confirmation targeted within 14 days.

A worked example

  • Day 1: M-TOC™ meets a veteran at a parish resource fair; triage opens a case — credit recovery plus housing risk flags.
  • Week 2: LCDC housing navigation takes the housing lane; benefits and claims navigation opens in parallel.
  • Month 2: TOWED enrolls the veteran in a trades track; a Guardian Angels volunteer mentor checks in weekly.
  • Month 6: Boot Camp application scored, cohort seat offered with a scholarship underwritten through RV3-channeled sponsorship.
  • Month 12: venture launches from the SEISMIC™ pipeline with a micro-capital-ready package at a CDFI partner.
