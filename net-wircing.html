Strategic Communications Plan

A “surround sound” platform strategy

The consortium’s communications plan adopts an integrated, multi-channel approach so that consistent messaging reaches rural veterans across every platform — each channel reinforcing the others.

🌐

Website

The central hub for resources — benefits information, housing programs, training, partner links — with news, referral directories, event calendars, online sign-ups and applications, and analytics baked in.

📱

Mobile App

Extends services to smartphones: push notifications, interactive tools like a benefits calculator, offline access for low-connectivity areas, and real-time support through chat and hotline links. Tier 3–4 subscribers get gated app access.

🎤

Podcast

Regular episodes by and for veterans — storytelling and peer connection, because “bringing veterans to veterans is the best rehabilitation.” Every episode drives listeners back to the website and app to enroll.

The surround-sound effect: a website article on housing resources is supplemented by an app notification and a related podcast interview with a housing expert — one message, three channels, no veteran missed.

Measured Like an Enterprise

KPIs on every channel, crosswalked to outcomes

Net-WIRCing instruments everything it builds. Website KPIs: monthly unique visitors, time on site, resource downloads, sign-up conversions, referral click-throughs. App KPIs: installs, daily and monthly active users, Day-1 and Day-7 retention, push notification open rates, in-app referrals. Podcast KPIs: episodes produced, subscribers, downloads, completion rates, and listener growth.

A crosswalk maps each outcome metric to its channels: “# veterans engaged” is proxied by site visits, app logins, and podcast downloads; “# enrolled” by form completions and call-to-action conversions; “# referrals” by referral logs across every platform — integrated through Google Analytics, Firebase, and podcast host analytics.

The balanced scorecard

  • Engagement metrics — visitors, listeners, downloads.
  • Action metrics — sign-ups, referrals, applications.
  • Impact metrics — housing placements and benefit awards, linked back to the platform that produced them.

Impact rolls up to the ecosystem’s VI-PAR dashboard, so funders and partners see engagement become outcomes in one view.

Earned Revenue

B2B referral and consulting — technology that pays for the mission

Net-WIRCing is a working LLC, not a cost center. Its B2B line contracts with partner agencies and providers for referral routing, outreach, and capacity building — the same infrastructure that runs the consortium, offered as a service to organizations that need it. Together with subscriptions and sponsorship, this consulting revenue completes the ecosystem’s blended sustainability model: subscriptions fund predictable operating costs, sponsors underwrite high-impact benefits, and B2B consulting scales expertise while diversifying revenue.

What partners can contract

  • Referral-routing and intake workflow builds with consent and privacy controls embedded end-to-end.
  • WCAG-accessible, mobile-friendly website and progressive-web-app development.
  • CRM and case-workflow configuration with KPI dashboards aligned to VI-PAR.
  • Digital-literacy and IT training cohorts for staff and the communities they serve.
  • Analytics setup — Google Analytics, Firebase, and Google Ad Grants guidance (up to $10K/month in free ads for eligible nonprofits).

Build Timeline

Design, pilot, scale — the three-year platform plan

Year 1 — pilot in the Florida Parishes

Q1: finalize WCAG-accessible, mobile-friendly platform specifications and hire the core team. Q2: build the MVP website and app; record the first podcast series. Q3: user testing with local veterans and partners. Q4: official launch with a social media and local PR campaign, measuring initial KPIs.

Year 2 — scale statewide

Content for additional Louisiana parishes; deeper partnerships; enhanced app features such as geo-targeted alerts; sustained marketing and KPI optimization.

Year 3 — regional expansion

Expand to neighboring states, systematize operations, and formalize the replication “playbook” for potential franchising.

Core team: project manager, web/app developers, content creators, social media and podcast producers, a data analyst, a webmaster/IT specialist, a content editor, and outreach coordinators.
