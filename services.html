Subscription Services & Technical Assistance

One membership. Structured navigation. Measurable outcomes.

Veterans and their families routinely face a fragmented ecosystem: multiple agencies, duplicative paperwork, inconsistent guidance, and limited rural access. A subscription model provides predictable access, standardized workflows, and accountability — a national digital backbone with localized activation when hands-on help is required.

For Veterans & Families

Four tiers, equitable on-ramps

Tiered access aligns cost-to-serve with intensity while keeping meaningful navigation — and an upgrade path — at every level. Members include veterans and eligible dependents (spouse and dependents under 21), with first responders served where the network can render services.

Tier 1 · Scout Access

Entry

Orientation, resource navigation, group education, and basic referral routing — delivered through the national virtual backbone.

Tier 2 · Navigator Plus

Standard

Everything in Scout Access, plus case check-ins, claims support within program limits, and expanded referrals — virtual with limited local activation.

Tier 3 · Guardian Family

Family

Adds gated app and podcast access, emotional-support-animal (ESA) support included, and limited structural housing modifications — virtual plus local activation.

Tier 4 · VAPE Unlimited Premium

Premium

Unlimited access, the premium Veterans Action Plan for Empowerment (VAPE), relocation support, and full execution lanes — with priority local delivery for high-need cases.

Pricing note: tier pricing is finalized at platform launch with income-based on-ramps and sponsor-underwritten scholarships — the same equity logic as the Boot Camp. Ask to be notified when enrollment opens.

Compliance, non-negotiable

Claims representation is performed only by appropriately accredited representatives; coaching that is not representation is clearly labeled. Consent-to-share workflows and minimum-necessary data practices protect every member.

Quality assured

Documented SOPs, escalation protocols, periodic case-file reviews, and vetted partners held to service-level expectations with feedback loops.

Outcomes tracked

Claim readiness, reduced homelessness risk, legal preparedness, survivor benefit uptake, and VAPE plan execution — measured monthly, quarterly, and annually against the VI-PAR framework.

The collaboration framework

Join the Network

How organizations join the E-Force Multiplier Network

The E-Force Multiplier Network is the consortium’s formal partner fabric: providers, agencies, employers, and funders bound by MOUs, shared performance expectations, and closed-loop referrals.

Step 1 — Discovery conversation

A structured intake of your organization’s mission, services, capacity, and what you need from the network — the same onboarding pathway established in the EEC/LCDC overview and onboarding framework.

Step 2 — MOU and performance expectations

Partnership as infrastructure: a memorandum of understanding with defined referral lanes, service-level expectations, data-sharing consent protocols, and feedback loops.

Step 3 — Referral integration

Your intake connects to the consortium’s referral tracker; warm hand-offs are coordinated through CAPE, and closed-loop confirmation is targeted within 14 days.

Step 4 — Quarterly review and growth

Partner performance is reviewed quarterly alongside the consortium scorecard; strong partners gain co-branded outreach, event slots, and priority lanes in the sponsorship and procurement pipeline.

Companies Who Care4Veterans

Sponsorship that underwrites visible impact

The consortium’s blended revenue model has three legs: subscriptions fund predictable operating costs and core navigation; grants and sponsorships underwrite high-need pathways — housing stabilization, legal readiness, rural outreach, ESA support; and Net-WIRCing B2B consulting scales expertise while diversifying revenue.

Sponsors join the Companies Who Care4Veterans pipeline — 2026 targets call for twelve corporate sign-ons — with sponsorships and procurement partnerships built through discovery, not rate cards: we ask about your goals first and build the proposal with you.

What sponsors underwrite

  • M-TOC deployments — a branded front-door event in your community.
  • Boot Camp scholarships for high-barrier, high-readiness applicants.
  • Guardian Family benefits: ESA support and housing modifications.
  • Rural outreach clinics and partner office hours by parish hub.
